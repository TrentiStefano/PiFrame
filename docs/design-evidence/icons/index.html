Design Foundations

Architectural Geometry & Hierarchy

  • 1-Piece Cohesive Crust: The crust forms the continuous bottom foundation slab of the pie slice and wraps seamlessly up into the curved back fluted rim—zero gaps, zero floating parts.
  • Darker Tone for Crust: The crust is rendered in a slightly darker matte tone than the filling facet, providing physical ground weight.
  • Dead-Centered Pi Logo: Positioned at the exact surface centroid with balanced 33px margins to both the left and right edges, projected flat in true perspective.
  • Strict Palette Discipline: 100% Catppuccin tokens (Frappé & Latte) with signature Mauve used strictly as a hairline pinstripe accent.
