Design Directions & Icon Candidates

Option 1: The Artisan Baker's Stamp

Classic & Delicious
Frappé (Dark)
Latte (Light)

A top-down circular pie with a 16-flute crimped crust edge, radial pinch indentations, and warm pastry gradients. The official pi-coding-agent logo is precision-cut into the crust as the baker's steam vent, flanked by curved pastry slits.

  • Most evocative pastry identity — unmistakable 'pie crust' feel
  • Clever steam vent metaphor for code agent output
  • Rich warmth and artisan craft aesthetic
  • Warm golden crust paired with signature Catppuccin Mauve

Option 2: Modern Bento Squircle

Tech-First Desktop
Frappé (Dark)
Latte (Light)

Built specifically for desktop operating systems. A smooth squircle chassis holds a recessed pie plate with a modern 12-flute crimped lip. The pi-coding-agent logo floats at center, flanked by code angle brackets (< and >) doubling as crust vents.

  • Seamless fit next to VS Code, Terminal, and modern dev tools
  • Uses Pi-Crust's Bento design rules (1px subtle border, rounded surfaces)
  • High tech-to-pie balance: unmistakably developer-centric
  • Excellent dock & taskbar silhouette

Option 3: Lattice Matrix

Handcrafted Grid
Frappé (Dark)
Latte (Light)

Traditional woven lattice crust meets software engineering matrix. Overlapping diagonal pastry strips cross at 45 degrees within a fluted pie dish, surrounding a central pastry medallion stamped with the pi-coding-agent mark.

  • Visual metaphor of woven pastry strips matching code logic / matrix
  • High visual texture and tactile depth
  • Playful yet structured balance
  • Looks stunning on Retina displays

Option 4: Minimalist Fluted Seal

Max Legibility
Frappé (Dark)
Latte (Light)

Pure vector modernist badge. A crisp 12-lobed fluted crust silhouette with an inner circular well, bold high-contrast pi-coding-agent mark, and minimal vertical baker score marks.

  • Supreme legibility down to 16x16 px favicon and tray sizes
  • Zero gradient clutter — pure graphic silhouette
  • Swiss-modernist brand authority
  • High contrast in both bright daylight and dark IDEs

Option 5: Fluted Crust Cradle

Minimalist Dynamic
Frappé (Dark)
Latte (Light)

An open, modern composition. A fluted golden crust crescent cradles the pi-coding-agent logo from below like a warm dish, with delicate rising steam whisps above.

  • Dynamic upward energy and warmth
  • Open, airy silhouette that breaks away from closed badge shapes
  • Distinctive and memorable dock presence
